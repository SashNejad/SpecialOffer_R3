Questions

Before you book

If yours is not here, ask it on the call. It is free and there is no obligation.

I have never lifted weights. Is that a problem?

No. This coaching is built for people starting from zero. The first session covers the basics, and the plan starts at your level.

How will I know I am doing the exercises right?

We go through every exercise together in the first session. After that, send me a short video of any lift on WhatsApp and I will tell you what to adjust.

Where does the in-person session happen?

At your gym: your condo building's gym or the one you belong to, in Griffintown and central Montreal. If you are somewhere else, we do the first session by video.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Your program is built around what you have: a condo gym, a set of dumbbells at home, or just your bodyweight.

How long before I see results?

It depends on where you start. Many clients notice more energy and confidence in the first weeks. Physical change builds over the following months of consistent training.

What is the difference between the two plans?

Both give you a personalized workout program. Full coaching adds a first session in person at your gym and a weekly check-in, so I adjust the plan with you as you go. The workout plan is the program on its own, to follow by yourself.

What is the commitment?

The call is free, with no obligation. Both plans are month to month. If you commit to 3 months on either plan, your custom meal plan is included at no extra cost, and 3 months is a realistic time to learn the movements and build the habit.
